# AI Company OS — Product

## Vision

AI Company OS is a SaaS application where a business owner creates a virtual company staffed by specialized AI agents. The isometric office is the visual operating interface; the real product is orchestration, knowledge, connectors, permissions, and execution underneath.

**Core idea:** "Your AI workforce, organized like a real company."

## User Flows

1. Create a company and describe its business
2. Upload company knowledge and policies
3. Connect business tools through integrations/MCP
4. Configure AI agents and permissions
5. Give the company a high-level objective
6. Let an orchestrator delegate work to specialized agents
7. Review outputs and approve sensitive actions
8. Monitor tasks, activity, costs, errors, and results

## Departments

| Department | Example Agents |
|---|---|
| Operations | Operations Manager, Process Analyst, Procurement, Admin |
| Marketing | Marketing Manager, Content, SEO, Social Media, Brand, Campaign Analyst |
| Sales | Sales Manager, Lead Research, Outreach, CRM, Proposal, Sales Analyst |
| Finance | Finance Manager, Invoice, Cashflow, Financial Reporting |
| Customer & Communication | Customer Support, Email, Meeting, Communication, Customer Success |
| Technology | CTO, Developer, QA, Data, IT Support |

## CEO / Orchestrator

The CEO is the top-level orchestration layer:

```
User goal → CEO/Orchestrator → Plan → Tasks → Agent assignment → Execution
→ Review → Approval → Synthesis → Final result
```

## Demo Mode (Phase 1)

The prototype works without external API keys. **Nova Coffee GmbH** is the seeded demo company with simulated agents demonstrating delegation, completion, and approvals.

## Positioning

Avoid "AI employees." Frame as an **AI operating system for business**:

- Outcome → Orchestration → Automation → Visibility
- The virtual office is the interface
- The orchestration engine is the product
- The human remains the CEO
