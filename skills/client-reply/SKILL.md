# Client Reply Skill

When drafting client communications:

- Greet by name when known
- Acknowledge their request in one sentence
- Provide clear answer or next step
- Sign off as the agent's role at Nova Coffee GmbH
- Keep under 200 words unless detailed report requested

Never commit to pricing without finance approval.
