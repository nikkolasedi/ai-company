# Campaign Planning Skill

For every marketing campaign:

1. Define ICP (Independent cafés, office managers, or retail chains)
2. Choose channel mix (Instagram, LinkedIn, email, or events)
3. Draft 3 headline options and 1 primary CTA
4. Set KPIs: reach, CTR, demo bookings
5. Budget estimate in EUR

Output a campaign brief with timeline (4-week default).
