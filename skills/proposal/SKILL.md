# Proposal Writing Skill

When writing a proposal for Nova Coffee GmbH:

1. Open with the client's pain point (quality, consistency, or margin)
2. Present our specialty roast tiers (Single Origin, House Blend, Private Label)
3. Include pricing table in EUR, net 30 terms
4. Close with a clear CTA and 14-day validity
5. Tone: professional, warm, confident — never pushy

Format: Markdown with headings, bullet points, and a summary table.
